Credits & thanks

Stylo Starter is by Angie Hartwell / Byte-Sized Booksmith.

The toolkit was inspired by Future Fiction Academy's stylometry teaching, Elizabeth Ann West and team. Its code is an independent implementation of established stylometry methods, with acknowledgment of the learning that informed it.

This beginner refresh follows the human-first approach and HTML design developed for the Book Machine Starter Kit. It adds a small no-download exercise, simpler reports, and Markdown workflows that let the AI handle setup while the author keeps the choices.

The two demonstration passages are original fictional teaching samples made for this kit with AI assistance. They are not excerpts from reference authors. No FFA course files or Spark package are included.
