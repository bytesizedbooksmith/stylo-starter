Change one preference.

Then see how it feels.

You might want shorter explanations, fewer observations, more attention to dialogue, or separate notes for two pen names. You do not need to reconfigure the whole toolkit.

Copy this into your AI
Help me change just one thing about how we use Stylo: [describe it]. Suggest the smallest useful adjustment. Record my preference in Markdown and show me an HTML reading copy. Don't change the measurement definitions or rewrite my source passages.

Your voice notes belong to you

The AI can turn observations and your reactions into a short voice card. It should record only preferences you actually confirmed. The numbers alone cannot decide what you prefer.

Copy this into your AI
Help me review my provisional voice notes. Separate measured observations from preferences I actually expressed. Remove any rule I didn't agree to. Keep intentional exceptions. Give me the updated HTML reading page and Markdown card for future AI sessions.

You can change your mind next week, or want a different voice for a different story.
