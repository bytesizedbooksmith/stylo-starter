Byte-Sized Booksmith · a gentle look at your prose

Notice a pattern.
Keep your voice.

You do not need a backlist, a statistics lesson, or a perfect paragraph to begin.

Stylometry means measuring patterns in writing: things such as sentence lengths, paragraph shapes and punctuation. Stylo helps you notice those patterns and decide whether they matter to you.

Start with someone else's practice problem.

We included two fictional passages about the same moment. Read a few observations, see what you notice, and keep whatever feels useful. Neither passage is the answer you are supposed to choose.

You can enjoy a long, winding sentence and still want a shorter one in the next paragraph. You can like parts of both examples. Numbers leave plenty of room for being human.
