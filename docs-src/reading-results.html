A confrontation, a memory and a quiet ending may look quite different in the numbers. A character can sound controlled while feeling anything but controlled. Stylo cannot read the emotional truth for you.

Three layers stay separate

  1. Measured: what the script counted under its rules.
  2. Interpreted: what the AI thinks that might mean in this passage.
  3. Chosen: what you want to keep or try.

The software's English sentence rules and quotation handling can misread unusual formatting. More specialized language features in the full engine are labeled as estimates or proxies. Repeating a calculation consistently does not make every linguistic judgment correct.

What about SpaCy?

This kit uses Python with its built-in libraries. SpaCy is an additional Python library with trained components for parts of speech and sentence structure. It may be worth evaluating for deeper grammatical measurements, but it is not included or required here.

Changing engines would require checking the same passages and rebuilding compatible baselines. A tool being created with Claude or Codex does not establish its accuracy.
